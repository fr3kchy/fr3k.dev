02 Methodology

Three files define everything an agent is, knows, and does. This is what separates a real agent from a chatbot.

SOUL.md

Agent identity & behavioural framework. Name, role, voice, archetype, core behaviours, and boundaries. Its defined personality matches your brand.

MEMORY.md

Persistent context store. Episodic (what happened), semantic (what you know), procedural (how you work). Survives session resets — forever.

HEARTBEAT.md

Autonomous lifecycle engine. Loads identity, refreshes context, scans for inputs, prioritises, and acts — proactively, on a cycle, without a prompt.
