01 About

I build autonomous systems where models, tools, infrastructure, hardware, safety and operations have to agree. My work spans local-first agent infrastructure, resilient communications, distributed control planes, model evaluation and optimisation, RAG, multimodal generation, embodied AI, and evidence-gated field systems.

I do not count scaffolds as finished systems, and I do not report simulated passes as execution evidence.
