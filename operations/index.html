Fictional pilot walkthrough

Follow one shift into a payroll draft.

A fictional workshop uses one shift to rehearse a pay-period handoff. All times and records below are invented.

  1. Roster the shift. A coordinator assigns a workshop shift from 08:00 to 16:00 with a 30-minute unpaid break.
  2. Reconcile attendance. The worker records a 16:15 finish. The extra quarter-hour goes to a supervisor for review.
  3. Approve the timesheet. The supervisor checks the finish time and break, then approves 7 hours 45 minutes for the draft.
  4. Prepare the handoff. A payroll reviewer compares the draft hours with the approved shift record and checks the applicable pay rules before export.

The walkthrough ends at human review. Live tenant reconciliation, payroll sign-off and payment remain outside this pilot.
