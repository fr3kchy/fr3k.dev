fR3k flagship / Android control surface · Discord plugin · agent bridge

Turn Discord on Android into a live FR3K control surface.

CORTEX is a Revenge plugin for Discord Android paired with a Termux companion daemon. It exposes context menus and /fr3k commands, routes events to services and agents, and bridges the phone to FR3K hardware and infrastructure.
