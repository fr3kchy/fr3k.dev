01 WHAT BLACKWAV3 IS

BLACKWAV3 begins with a simple problem:

different devices should not be forced into fake uniformity.

A T-Deck Plus has a keyboard and LoRa radio. A K10 has cameras, microphones and sensors. A K230 has much heavier edge-compute potential. A T-Echo is useful because it can remain small and low-power. A Linux machine belongs in infrastructure.

BLACKWAV3 allows each of them to specialize while exposing a common system boundary.

CAPABILITY EARNED BY EVIDENCE · DISCOVERY IS NOT AUTHORITY · THE NETWORK IS NOT THE SYSTEM

⟁

CAPABILITY OVER MODEL NAME

Hardware is represented by what is actually present and usable, not merely by a product label.

◈

DISCOVERY IS NOT AUTHORITY

A device being visible does not imply permission to invoke sensitive capabilities.

⟡

TRANSPORT IS A SERVICE

Applications specify the behaviour they need instead of hardcoding LoRa, Wi-Fi, BLE, cellular or USB.

⬢

EVIDENCE BEFORE CLAIMS

A compile, hardware probe, flash, boot and physical validation are different evidence states.
